Our Story & About Us

A quiet pause along the Ahmedabad–Palanpur stretch.

IMMY's COFFEE CAFE was born from a simple wish: to create a warm, unhurried neighborhood sanctuary right at Toran Club where travelers passing along the highway and locals from Palanpur feel equally at home.

We believe a great cafe isn't about complicated menus or rushed service—it's about the comforting aroma of freshly ground beans, a golden grilled sandwich shared between old friends, and a welcoming table where you can linger over your cup without watching the clock.

Whether you're stopping in for a morning masala chai, catching up on work in a quiet corner, or winding down after sunset, our doors—and our espresso machine—are always warmed up for you.

“Good coffee warms the hands; a good cafe warms the day. Welcome to our corner at Toran Club.”

— Team IMMY's Coffee Cafe

The Ambience at IMMY's

Thoughtfully designed to slow down the pace of the highway outside—combining warm textures, gentle lighting, and unhurried comfort.

Warm Amber Glow

Soft overhead pendant lamps cast a golden, soothing light across every table—easy on the eyes from late afternoon into the night.

Earthy Wood & Greenery

Hand-finished wooden tables, plush corner booths, and lush indoor plants bring calm, organic warmth to every seat.

Soft Acoustic Vibes

Curated lo-fi and acoustic instrumental melodies play gently in the background—ideal for reading, quiet chats, or remote work.
